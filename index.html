                Backup and restore

                Portable backup

                JSON is a portable plain-text backup of your structured Minimal data. It is not encrypted; store exports privately.

                Do not put passwords, financial credentials, authentication codes, or other secrets in Minimal. Closing Minimal does not normally erase data, but clearing website/app data, browser deletion, storage eviction, or private-browsing closure may. Localhost and the GitHub Pages version use separate browser storage.

                Type DELETE MINIMAL to permanently clear local records.

                Privacy

                Privacy & safety

                • Without Sync, all personal data stays on this phone. With Sync on, a copy is kept on Minimal's Cloudflare server. It is protected by your passcode but is not end-to-end encrypted.
                • Minimal never requests contacts, location, notifications, camera, microphone, or banking access.
                • External launcher links leave Minimal and follow the destination’s privacy rules.
                • Backups include settings and historical snapshots and are unencrypted plain text.
